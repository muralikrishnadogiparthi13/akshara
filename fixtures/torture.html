Akshara torture test

Every bug the scanner looks for, reproduced deliberately. Left is broken, right is correct.

1 · Vertical clipping

line-height: 1.05
शुभ विवाह की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ
line-height: 1.65
शुभ विवाह की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ

Devanagari's shirorekha sits above Latin cap-height and the vowel signs stack above that. A line-height chosen for English shears the tops off.

2 · Font fallback

Latin-only webfont
Wedding · विवाह
a stack that covers the script
Wedding · विवाह

The English renders in the brand face and the Hindi in whatever the phone has. Different weight, different x-height, same sentence.

3 · Markup cutting through a letter

highlight splits the cluster
संस्कृति क्षेत्र
highlight respects the boundary
संस्कृति क्षेत्र

Shaping does not cross an element boundary. Chrome’s line breaker will never split an akshara — measured, not assumed — but a <mark> landing mid-cluster stops the conjunct forming.

A vowel sign decorating empty space. Damage in every Brahmic script, without exception.

7 · Deprecated encodings and numerals

U+0958 + mixed digits
क़िला — २० November 2026
canonical + one numeral system
क़िला — 20 November 2026

क़ exists twice in Unicode. Both cells render identically and compare as different bytes, so search misses one and deduplication keeps both.

8 · Correct by construction

வணக்கம், தமிழ்
നമസ്കാരം, മലയാളം
ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ಕನ್ನಡ
নমস্কার, বাংলা

Tamil and Malayalam end words on a bare pulli or chandrakkala. A scanner that calls this truncation condemns two entire languages — nothing in this block should be reported.
